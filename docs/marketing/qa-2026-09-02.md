Owner: storefront (frontend & growth). Scope: every public route of the
consumer app (`frontend/src/public/**`), desktop 1440×900 and mobile
390×844, light and dark, signed out / fresh / returning / exhausted /
service-down / service-hanging.

**Validation level: FIXTURE_CONFIRMED.** Every screenshot below was taken
by `frontend/tests/visual-qa.mjs` against the built bundle served locally,
with Supabase replaced by an in-process fixture router (host

exercised up to the `signInWithOtp` request.

