# Short-form scripts — TikTok / Instagram Reels (Sept 2026)

Three 15–30 second scripts, each built on the agent submitting a **real**
application live. Shot lists and on-screen text included. Companion to
`college-launch.md` §1.5 (format evidence) and §5 (why these post the
week of Sep 8).

Rules these scripts obey — they are the product's house rules wearing a
marketing hat:

- **Only real runs.** Every clip of the agent is a screen recording of an
  actual application on a real employer site, with the receipt the product
  stored. No mock-ups, no staged "submitted" screens, no speeding up a
  fill so it looks faster than it is (a cut is fine, a time-warp is not —
  say "sped up" on screen if you must).
- **No numbers we can't show.** No user counts, no "students at 40
  campuses", no testimonials. The one stat we quote is the real Sep 1 run:
  **7 applications across 5 job boards in one day**, receipts on file.
- **Redact before you post.** The screen recording shows the operator's
  own profile going into forms. Blur or crop name, email, phone, address,
  resume filename, and any screener answer. The receipt screenshot in the
  dashboard gets the same treatment. The product does not blur for you.
- **Never show a demographic / EEO section being filled.** Those fields
  fill only from the operator's encrypted self-ID profile; they are not
  content. Cut around them.
- **Never imply it sends email as you.** It drafts; you send. If an
  outreach draft appears on screen, the caption says "drafted for me to
  send".
- **Invite-only means invite-only.** The CTA is either a personal invite
  link (`/redeem?code=JRA-XXXX-XXXX`) or the waitlist (`/#waitlist`).
  Don't promise instant access.

Why this format: struggle-and-receipts content with the hook inside the
first 3 seconds is what performs on CareerTok
(https://www.theleap.co/blog/careertok-tiktok-trend/,
https://www.worklife.news/talent/tik-tok-job-application/); the viral
reference point is Wonsulting's "4,000 applications, 60 interviews, 1
offer" (https://www.tiktok.com/@jerryjhlee/video/7499616797478374698).
Our twist is that the receipts are literal.

Production notes common to all three:

- Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920. Screen recordings of the console/dashboard
  are 16:9 — crop to the region that matters (the form field being
  filled, the submit button, the receipt), don't letterbox the whole
  desktop.
- Captions burned in (most viewers are on mute in a lecture hall, which
  is also the joke).
- On-screen text: two lines max at a time, top third of the frame so
  the TikTok UI doesn't cover it.
- End card is identical on all three (below) so the CTA is
  recognisable across the series.
- File the source recording and the receipt PNG next to the export so
  anyone can check the clip against the artifact.

**Shared end card (last 2–3 s):**

> on screen: `dispatch · invite-only · receipts for everything`
> second line: `link in bio → waitlist` (or `your invite: JRA-XXXX-XXXX`
> when a strategist posts with a personal code)

## Script 1 — "While I was in lecture" (≈22 s)

Hook: the applications happen during the one hour you can't be at your
laptop. Format idea #2 from `college-launch.md` §1.5.

| t | Shot | Audio / VO | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Phone, handheld, from a lecture-hall seat: the slide deck, the back of heads, the clock on the wall. | (ambient lecture) VO, deadpan: "This is my 10 a.m." | `10:04 am · lecture` |
| 3–6 s | Hard cut: screen recording, the console run timeline with a job title and company visible (everything else blurred). Cursor isn't moving — the agent is. | VO: "This is also my 10 a.m." | `10:04 am · my laptop, at home` |
| 6–14 s | Screen recording, cropped tight: the agent filling three fields in a row on a real Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby form — school, graduation year, "why do you want to work here" (a real profile answer, blurred after the first line). Real speed. | VO: "It fills the forms from a profile I wrote once. My words — it doesn't make anything up." | `fills from *my* profile` → `no guessing` |
| 14–18 s | Screen recording: the submit click, the confirmation page, then the dashboard row flipping to *submitted* with the receipt button. | VO: "And it keeps a screenshot of every single one." | `screenshot receipt · stored` |
| 18–20 s | Back to the phone in the lecture hall. Look at the phone, small nod, back to the slides. | VO: "Receipts. While I'm here." | `7 applications · 5 job boards · one day (Sep 1, real run)` |
| 20–22 s | End card. | (music out) | shared end card |

Shot list:

1. Lecture hall B-roll, 10 s, phone, landscape is fine — crop to
   vertical later. Get the wall clock if there is one.
2. Console run timeline, screen recording, 30 s, with the actual run
   from that hour (`artifacts/console/run-timeline.html` view or the
   dashboard — whichever shows the timestamps).
3. Form fill, screen recording, full length of one real application;
   pull three consecutive fields.
4. Submit + confirmation + dashboard row, one continuous recording.
5. Receipt PNG opened from the dashboard for a 1-second insert if the
   row flip is too small to read.

Caption (post text): "It applied to 7 jobs on 5 different job boards
while I was in lecture, and I have the screenshot for every one.
Invite-only right now — waitlist in bio. #internship #summer2027
#jobsearch #careertok"

## Script 2 — "The 3-minute submit, in 30 seconds" (≈28 s)

Hook: prove it's real. One application, start to receipt, no cuts
inside the fill except honest jump cuts with a visible timer. Format
idea #1 from §1.5.

| t | Shot | Audio / VO | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Screen recording: a real job posting's apply page (company name and role visible, nothing else). A stopwatch overlay starts at 0:00. | VO: "Watch an agent apply to a real job. I'm not going to touch the keyboard." | `real posting · real form` / `timer is real` |
| 3–8 s | Same recording: name / email / phone fields fill (blurred), resume attaches — filename blurred, the attach animation visible. | VO: "Contact info, resume —" | `⏱ 0:14` |
| 8–14 s | Jump cut with a hard whip-transition and the timer jumping (e.g. 0:14 → 1:05): work-authorization screener chosen from the page's own options; a "how did you hear about us" dropdown. | VO: "— the screener questions it answers from what I told it, and only that. Anything it doesn't know becomes a to-do, not a guess." | `answers only from my profile` → `unknown = to-do, not a guess` |
| 14–20 s | Jump cut, timer 1:05 → 2:40: the long "tell us about yourself" box filling with real text (blur after line one). | VO: "Essays are drafted from a page I wrote about myself and checked before they go in." | `from my about-me page` |
| 20–25 s | Timer 2:51: Submit. Confirmation page. Then the dashboard: the new row, *submitted*, receipt button, click it, the screenshot opens. | VO: "Submitted. And there's the receipt — the actual screenshot it stored." | `⏱ 2:51 · submitted` → `receipt: the screenshot it kept` |
| 25–28 s | End card. | VO: "Every application. Every time." | shared end card |

Shot list:

1. One complete screen recording of one application, unbroken, with a
   real stopwatch overlay (OBS or the OS recorder plus a timer window).
   Keep the unbroken original; the cut version links to it in the
   caption if anyone asks.
2. Dashboard receipt open, recorded separately if the first recording
   ends at the confirmation page.
3. No B-roll. This one lives or dies on being obviously unedited
   inside each segment.

Notes: pick a posting with a short form (Lever or Ashby — the inspect
reports under `artifacts/ats-inspect/` say which forms are short).
Greenhouse with a long custom-question set makes a worse video. If the
run stops on a to-do (a question the profile doesn't answer), **keep
it** — "it stopped and asked me instead of guessing" is a better
15 seconds than a clean submit, and it's true.

Caption: "One real application, one real timer, zero keystrokes from me.
The screenshot at the end is what it stores for every submission.
Invite-only — waitlist in bio."

## Script 3 — "The ritual" (≈18 s)

Hook: the shared pain. 40 seconds of retyping your work history into a
form that clears itself, compressed to 10, then the cut. Format idea #4
from §1.5. Wry, not salesy.

| t | Shot | Audio / VO | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Phone, over-the-shoulder at a laptop, 11 pm desk light. The cursor in "Employer name" on a real careers form. | (typing sounds) | `application #14 this week` |
| 2–9 s | Sped-up (say so) typing of the same job history — company, title, dates, description — into the form. Whip-pan every 2 s to a *different* company's form, same fields, same typing. Four forms. | VO, tired: "Same job. Same dates. Same four bullet points. Fourteenth time." | `sped up 8×` (persistent, small) / `same history, every form` |
| 9–11 s | Session-timeout modal or a form that reset (real; every ATS has one). Hands leave the keyboard. | (silence) | `…and it cleared.` |
| 11–16 s | Hard cut: screen recording of the agent filling the same work-history block on a real form in one pass, then the dashboard receipt. | VO, normal voice: "I wrote it once. It types it every time. And it shows me the screenshot." | `written once` → `typed every time` → `screenshot receipt` |
| 16–18 s | End card. | (music sting) | shared end card |

Shot list:

1. Desk B-roll at night, phone, 20 s, the real forms on screen (blur the
   typed content in post — it's the operator's real history).
2. Four different careers pages open in tabs; record 10 s of typing into
   each. Real sites, real forms, no submit (this is the "before" — do
   not submit anything by hand for a video).
3. One real timeout / cleared-form moment. If you can't catch one
   organically, drop the beat rather than fake it.
4. Agent filling the employment section on a real form, screen
   recording, 20 s, plus the receipt open.

Caption: "Fourteen applications, fourteen times typing the same job
history. Then I gave it to an agent. It keeps a screenshot of every
submission so I can check its work. Invite-only — waitlist in bio."

## Posting plan and what to measure

- Post Script 3 first (pain, broadest), Script 1 second (lifestyle),
  Script 2 third (proof, for the skeptics the first two bring in) — the
  Sep 8–14 launch week in `college-launch.md` §5.
- Same video on TikTok and Reels; Reels gets the caption trimmed to two
  lines.
- Measure **waitlist joins** and **invite redemptions**, not views.
  The waitlist table records the email only, so attribution is the bio
  link's UTM (or a per-strategist invite code when we have them —
  storefront report, referral view ask). Watch the 3-second hold rate
  to pick which hook gets the follow-ups.
- Replies to "is this real?" get the unbroken recording from Script 2
  and the receipt PNG, not an argument.
