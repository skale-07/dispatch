Software Engineer (Full-time, part-time, or Intern)

Be part of a team passionate about building a great auto-remediation platform

About Us :

We are a startup founded by ex-Amazon and Intel employees.

Neptune.io is SaaS platform that auto-remediates IT monitoring alerts. Neptune sits on top of existing monitoring systems such as Amazon CloudWatch, NewRelic etc, and takes automated actions when alerts come, thus improving DevOps productivity and saving costs.

Careers at Neptune.io :

We know you are already working at large companies, but some of you are passionate about starting up on your own in future. The best way to launch a startup is to take part in a top-notch startup and learn it all first hand ! Currently, we are on a mission to build an amazing platform for developers. If you are excited about building a scalable distributed system ground-up, this is an amazing opportunity. Come join us!

Software Engineer, Distributed systems (Full-time or Part-time or Intern)

Requirements:

  • Expert software development experience in Java
  • Extensive knowledge of Unix/Linux
  • Experience with AWS EC2, AWS S3, DynamoDB is a plus
  • Familiarity with distributed data management, design/implementation tradeoffs in building high-performance, fault-tolerant distributed systems
  • Education: BS/ MS/PhD in Computer Science or related field
  • Most important of all, you enjoy building a bootstrapped startup from ground-up

Responsibilities:

  • Building distributed systems software
  • Improving performance and scalability
  • Diagnosing and debugging complex issues
  • Recruit and build the team with rockstars like you

What you'll get from us

  • Opportunity to work along a top-notch team that has experience in companies like Amazon, Intel and Boston Consulting Group. The team has built and shipped services like Amazon S3 and Amazon DynamoDB
  • Learn what's involved in launching a startup - talking to customers, iterating quickly, delivering production-ready code and building the team
