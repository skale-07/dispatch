Privacy Statement


IMPORTANT INFORMATION REGARDING PROTECTION OF CANDIDATE PERSONAL INFORMATION, PLEASE READ AND AGREE TO THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS DESCRIBED IN THIS PRIVACY POLICY FOR CANDIDATES BEFORE SUBMITTING ANY PERSONAL INFORMATION ON BEHALF OF A CANDIDATE


At Textron we know you care about your personal privacy. Because we care, we have adopted the practices described in this Privacy Policy for Candidates. You should not access Textron’s recruiting website (this “Site”) or use our services until you have carefully read this Privacy Policy for Candidates and agree to the terms and conditions described in it.


Textron's Privacy Policy for Candidates (”Privacy Policy for Candidates”)


Textron Inc. and its direct and indirect subsidiaries, divisions, segments, and business units (collectively “Textron”) respects the privacy of Candidates, applicants, and employees, and is committed to complying with applicable privacy laws in every country in which we operate. This Privacy Policy for Candidates is intended to inform you about how the information you submit voluntarily as a job applicant (“Personal Data”) through this Site and throughout the Textron hiring process will be handled and protected by Textron. This policy supplements the general Privacy Policy posted on Textron.com.


Why We Collect Your Personal Data


Textron's hiring process consists of multiple steps designed to evaluate your skills and qualifications for a particular position.  When you choose to voluntarily submit a job application as part of the Textron hiring process you will be asked to provide Textron with Personal Data about yourself. The Personal Data that you provide will be used to evaluate your skills and requirements with respect to the job you apply for and, as set out below, match your skills and interest to Textron job requirements, as well as to comply with applicable laws and regulations related to the evaluation of those seeking employment. Your Personal Data will also be used to contact and identify you throughout the hiring process and relevant portions of your Personal Data, if you are hired, will be used to establish a basic employment record.  The Personal Data you provide may also be used to identify other positions that may be of interest to you within the Textron businesses.


Disclosures of Your Personal Data


Disclosures of and access to your Personal Data will be restricted to those Textron staff and designated third party agents who have a need to know the specific information in question in order to carry out their responsibilities with regard to Textron’s hiring process.  The information you provide in your application may be shared within the Textron organization to identify job opportunities that may fit the skills and experience you include.
We do not reveal Personal Data about you to unaffiliated third parties, unless: (1) you request or authorize it; (2) the information is provided to help complete a transaction for you; (3) the information is provided to comply with the law (such as equal opportunity laws and regulations), applicable regulations, court orders or subpoenas, to enforce our Terms of Use or other agreements, or to protect our rights, property or safety or the rights, property or safety of our users or others (e.g., to a consumer reporting agency for fraud protection etc.); (4) the disclosure is done as part of a purchase, transfer or sale of services or assets (e.g., in the event that substantially all of our assets are acquired by another party); or (5) the information is provided to our third party agents, outside vendors or service providers to perform functions on our behalf (this may include, but is not limited to administrating relocation benefits and conducting pre-employment screening). We may also gather aggregated data about you and disclose such aggregated (but not personally identifiable) information to third parties for analysis or other purposes.
Your information may be disclosed to governmental entities in compliance with applicable law, such as to those agencies authorized to review and enforce equal opportunity laws.

Collection and Storage of Data about You


Textron collects, stores, and may share your Personal Data with other Textron businesses.  The Personal Data that you provide to us through the Site, including but not limited to information on your resume, entered into your profile, or submitted on your application.  We may also collect information about you from other third parties, in order to: (a) verify information about your credentials, such as education and prior employment, (b) follow-up on references that you may provide, and (c) conduct background investigations, where permitted by local law. It is Textron's policy to collect such information from third parties only with your knowledge and agreement. Should Textron wish to obtain such third-party information about you, and you have not completed an employment profile authorizing us to do so, we will contact you and request your authorization to proceed. The third-party servers reside in the United States.

 
Your Rights to Your Personal Data


Once you have established a profile through this site, you will have online access to correct or update the information it contains, at any time.

 

For Candidate’s residing within the EU, according to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (hereinafter GDPR), you can:


• Access and obtain a copy of your Personal Data upon request
• Correct or update your Personal Data at any time, in which you have online access
• Request deletion of your Personal Data, for example, when the data is no longer necessary for the purpose of processing, for example, if you are not selected for the job for which you apply and/or if you no longer wish to be considered for job opportunities.


Retention and Deletion of Your Personally Data


Textron retains your Personal Data only as long as is necessary for evaluation for employment, as well as to comply with applicable laws relating to the evaluation of those seeking employment.


For Candidates Residing in the United States and Outside the EU:  In the event you are unsuccessful with the position applied for or if we have no suitable current positions matching your requirements, we will retain your Personal Data for a period of up to 24 months for consideration against future vacancies.


For Candidates Residing in the EU:  Your profile, including Personal Data, will be retained only as long as necessary to complete the evaluation for employment for the position you have applied for a period up to 6 months, if not otherwise notified by you.  

 


Safeguards


Textron uses reasonable administrative, technical, personnel, and physical measures to safeguard your Personally Data against loss, theft, and unauthorized use or modification. Textron takes reasonable steps to ensure that its vendors have similar practices in place.


International Data Transfers


Textron is a global company. As of May 25, 2018, the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) is effective.  This provides, among other things, extended information obligations for those responsible for data privacy. Therefore, Textron will inform you – in compliance with the new legal regulations – about the processing of your personal data.  Accordingly, Personal Data about Candidates may be transferred and processed in the US and other countries that may not be deemed to provide the same level of data protection as your home country. Textron maintains and applies data protection standards consistent with those specified in this Notice to its operations globally.  As part of its commitment to privacy, Textron adheres to strict data privacy principles to protect personal data. With respect to information about Candidates, Textron complies with any and all applicable national and local data protection laws. In addition, Textron has also committed to Inter-Company Data Processing Agreements based on the EU Model Contracts ((“Model Contracts” (also known as the Standard Contractual Clauses)), to authorize worldwide transfer, and subsequent sub-Processing, of Personal Data throughout global network of Textron Businesses.


When submitting your profile and application, Textron may transfer your Personal Data to the United States to store and process that data in accordance with this Privacy Policy for Candidates.


 Communications from Textron


  Textron may contact you, using the contact information you have provided, for purposes of responding to your application for a particular job or jobs.  In addition, Candidates may opt-in to be notified about new jobs matching your preferences.
