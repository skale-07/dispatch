These rights are limited and do not stop all disclosures of personal information. To exercise these rights, individuals in those states should follow these two steps: 1. You only need to complete this form once. 2. To opt out for personal information collected via cookies and similar technology on this Gulfstream website, , switch the “Sale” of Personal Data via Cookies or “Sharing”/“Processing” by Cookies for Targeted Ads toggle below to OFF (grey color) by moving it LEFT (if it’s not already there) and click “Confirm My Choice” below. This toggle affects cookie-based data for this Gulfstream website on this browser only, and you will need to repeat this process if you use another browser, if you clear your cookies, or if you use a browser that automatically clears cookies. Cookie-based data disclosures and processing from other Gulfstream websites you may visit, such as careers.gulfstream.com, are controlled through tools available in the footer of those sites, and you should opt out on those sites using those tools.
